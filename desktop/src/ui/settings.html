Welcome, Timmy

Two quick things and you're in.

More options (overlay hotkeys, screenshots, startup)

Overlay

A mini-map that floats over Tarkov. Set Tarkov's screen mode to Borderless (Settings → Graphics) or it can't show on top.

Position sharing

In raid, press your in-game screenshot key (Print Screen by default). Tarkov names the screenshot after your location, and Timmy reads it. Nothing touches the game itself.
