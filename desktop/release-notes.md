## Stash scanner

Out of raid, open your stash and press your normal **Tarkov screenshot key**. Tarkov Timmy reads the items off the picture and opens **Stash → Scanned stash**:

- Every item outlined by verdict: **keep** (your squad's quests or next hideout upgrade need it), **needed later**, **valuable**, **safe to sell**.
- Shows which items are **found in raid** (the ✓), and warns when a quest needs found-in-raid but that copy isn't.
- A **sell list** grouped by trader / flea, with totals.
- Unsure items are flagged with dashed red outlines. Click one to pick the right item; the app remembers your correction.

Everything runs on your PC; the screenshot is never uploaded. The first scan downloads the text-recognition model (~10 MB) and item icons once.
